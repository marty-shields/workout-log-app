The Dashboard now

This week

The week so far, day by day

The Dashboard becomes This week, Monday to Sunday. Each figure is compared with last week up to the same weekday, as the comparison table does today.

Starting a workout

+ starts the timer

The + is on both tabs and opens the workout screen you have today. The full-width New Workout button goes.

Past workouts and exercises

Opened from what you tap

The Workouts and Exercises lists are no longer places you go to. A past workout and an exercise's page open from the thing you tapped, with a back arrow that returns there.

Past workouts

A day in the month calendar, a week row or a bar in Trends opens the workouts for those dates. This is how you edit or delete an older workout. This week's workouts open straight from This week.

Exercises

A record or a Most trained row opens the exercise's page. The exercise list stays only as the picker behind Add exercise.

What you give up

An exercise you haven't trained lately and that holds no recent record has no route to its page.

Trends

How a week, month or year went

Week, month and year, with arrows to step back. Workouts, time, sets and volume lead each one, compared with the period before up to the same point. Scroll inside a phone to see the lower cards.

Sets

Strength sets only, as the Dashboard counts them today. A cardio session adds to workouts and time.

Volume

What counts towards volume

Volume is weight × reps, added up over the period. The app has six kinds of exercise and only some record a weight you lift for reps, so each kind needs its own rule. Shown in kilograms for a week or month and tonnes for a year.

KindWhat a set recordsCounts asWhy
Lift
228 exercises, e.g. Bench press		Weight and repsWeight × repsThe standard measure. The weight is counted as you entered it; a dumbbell weight is not doubled.
Bodyweight
56, e.g. Pull-up, Dip		Reps, with optional added weightAdded weight × repsThe app doesn't know your body weight. A weighted dip counts its plates; an unweighted pull-up adds nothing.
Assisted
3, e.g. Assisted pull-up		Reps and the assistanceNothingThe number is help from the machine, so more of it means less lifted.
Hold
6, e.g. Plank		Time, with optional weightNothingThere are no reps to multiply by.

6, e.g. Farmer's walk		Distance, time, optional weightNothingWeight × distance is a different measure and can't be added to weight × reps.
Cardio
16, e.g. Treadmill run		Time, distance and othersNothingNo weight is lifted.

What the tile says

The tile is labelled "strength" because the total covers lifts and added weight only. Work it leaves out still shows in workouts, time and sets.

Comparing periods

The same rules apply to both periods, so the change is like for like. A week with more bodyweight or cardio work will show lower volume without being an easier week.

What changes

The work in the app

AreaChange
Bottom barThree slots: This week, a raised + and Trends. New plus and bar-chart icons. The + goes to /workout/new.
Workouts and ExercisesBoth tabs are removed. Workouts opens only from Trends, filtered to the tapped dates, with a back arrow. The Exercises list is no longer a screen of its own; it stays as the Add exercise picker. Exercise pages open from records, Most trained and muscle group rows.
This weekReplaces the Dashboard: day strip, three tiles, this week's workouts, records this week, sets per muscle group for the week. The last workout card, 12-week bars and New Workout button are removed. The Muscles screen is no longer needed.
TrendsNew screen: Week, Month, Year switch, period stepper, four tiles, paired day bars, month calendar, month bars.
TotalsNew Core service giving workouts, time, sets, volume, records and sets per muscle group for any week, month or year and the one before it. Today's rules are fixed at 12 weeks, 7 days and 30 days.
VolumeNew calculation, by exercise kind as in the table above: weight × reps for lifts, added weight × reps for bodyweight moves, nothing for the rest.
CopyThe Workouts empty state points at the Dashboard's New Workout button; it should point at +. The navigation table in the design system is rewritten.
